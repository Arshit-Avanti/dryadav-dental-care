# Clinic Content & Clinical Integrity Guidelines

This skill enforces strict clinical, factual, ethical, and regulatory integrity across all website content, medical copywriting, and interface elements for healthcare and dental practices.

## Mandatory Rules of Integrity

When writing, reviewing, updating, or generating any content for **Dr Yadav Dental Care Point** or any clinical website:

### 1. Doctor Qualifications & Clinical Credentials
- **NEVER fabricate degrees, certifications, fellowships, or dental accreditations.**
- Only display degrees and credentials that are verified and explicitly approved by the practitioner (e.g., *Dr. Jagesh Yadav, BDS, MIDA*).
- Never claim unrecognized or non-existent surgical specialties.
- If additional fellowship, laser, implant, or orthodontic credentials are not verified, do not invent them.

### 2. Clinical Experience & Practice History
- **NEVER fabricate years of clinical practice or arbitrary surgery counts.**
- State documented experience accurately (e.g., *12+ years of clinical practice*).
- Do not make unsubstantiated statistical claims (e.g., "100,000 successful surgeries performed") unless corroborated by clinic logbooks.

### 3. Patient Testimonials & Google Ratings
- **NEVER invent fake patient stories, synthetic quotes, or staged reviews.**
- Real reviews must correspond to authentic patient feedback (e.g., verified Google Business profile reviews with actual patient consent and realistic treatment contexts).
- Never manufacture 5.0-star averages or fabricated rating badges without linking or attributing to genuine public profiles (Google Maps, Practo, Justdial).

### 4. Clinic Timings, Address & Contact Details
- **NEVER fabricate or guess opening hours, emergency numbers, or geographic landmarks.**
- Ensure physical clinic address matches verified municipal/Google Maps records:
  - *Dr. Yadav Dental Care Point, Opposite Sunrise Public School, 18, MM Road, DLF Ankur Vihar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201102*.
- Verified primary hotline: `+91 70655 77070`.
- Verified operating schedule:
  - *Monday: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM, 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM*
  - *Tuesday – Saturday: 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM*
  - *Sunday: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM*
- Never promise "24/7 round-the-clock emergency hospital walk-in" unless on-call arrangements are confirmed.

### 5. Medical & Dental Claims
- **NEVER invent absolute clinical guarantees.**
  - Avoid illegal or unethical claims like *"100% permanent pain-free guarantee"* or *"Zero-risk surgery forever"*.
  - Frame treatments honestly and ethically: *"computerized buffered micro-anesthesia for virtually painless injections"*, *"atraumatic extraction technique to minimize post-operative swelling"*.
- All clinical terminology must adhere to standard Dental Council of India (DCI) and Indian Dental Association (IDA) codes of ethics.

### 6. Handling Unverified Clinic Information & Placeholders
- Whenever clinic-specific information is missing:
  - Clearly identify the gap to the team or client.
  - Insert an explicit, easily editable placeholder token (e.g., `[PLACEHOLDER: Clinic License Number]` or `[VERIFY: Sunday Evening Slot]`).
  - Never fill unknown clinical facts with plausible-sounding hallucinations.

### 7. Patient-Friendly, Empathetic & Medically Responsible Language
- Write with warm, reassuring empathy that alleviates dental anxiety (odontophobia).
- Demystify procedures (explain what a Root Canal Treatment, Ultrasonic Scaling, or Ceramic Crown actually involves) without patronizing or frightening patients.
- Maintain clear aftercare transparency and emergency escalation instructions.

## Verification Checklist Before Publishing

1. [ ] Are all doctor names, qualifications, and associations exactly matching client approval?
2. [ ] Are patient quotes genuine or clearly labeled if approved case illustrations?
3. [ ] Are clinic address, phone numbers, WhatsApp routing, and opening hours tested and verified?
4. [ ] Are all medical claims free from deceptive guarantees and compliant with local healthcare advertising laws?
5. [ ] Are any pending or unverified items explicitly marked with editable placeholders?
